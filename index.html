Get to know me

Hey there! My name is Allen Kpentey. 👋🏽

I'm an electrical engineer, researcher, photographer, and designer driven by one simple belief: the best solutions happen when creativity meets engineering.

Whether I'm building a solar-assisted electric vehicle, developing renewable energy systems, conducting power electronics research, or capturing stories through a camera lens, I'm motivated by the same goal—to create technology and experiences that improve people's lives.

As a Research Assistant at Ashesi University, my work focuses on renewable energy integration, electric mobility, and power electronics. Outside the lab, I founded AK Studio, where I use photography and visual storytelling to help individuals and organizations communicate ideas with clarity and impact.

I enjoy working at the intersection of engineering, design, and entrepreneurship because I believe innovation isn't just about solving technical problems—it's about making those solutions accessible, meaningful, and inspiring.

I'm constantly learning, building, and exploring new ideas, with long-term interests in sustainable energy systems, robotics, and intelligent technologies that can help shape a more resilient future for Africa.

Completed View project

AURRA Solar-Assisted EV

Project Brief:

Designed and built a solar-assisted electric vehicle prototype for affordable clean mobility in Ghana. I led the integration of two 500 W BLDC hub motors, a lithium-ion battery system, 200 W solar charging, embedded sensing, and regenerative-braking concepts. The project achieved an average test speed of about 30.6 km/h and later became the basis of my Falling Walls Lab Accra Audience Award pitch, “Breaking the Wall of Affordable Clean Mobility.”

Project Info

  • Date - 2023 - 2024
  • Category - Electric Vehicles, Renewable Energy, Power Electronics
  • Tools - Arduino/ESP32, BLDC motors, Li-ion battery, CAD, sensors
  • Role - Lead designer and builder
  • Recognition - Falling Walls Lab Accra Audience Award
Deployed View project

Solar-Powered Smart Bench

Project Brief:

Contributed to the implementation and evaluation of an off-grid solar-powered smart bench deployed at Ashesi University. The system combines a 1.1 kWp PV array, 1.5 kW inverter, and 2.56 kWh lithium-ion battery to provide charging access for up to six users. My work included system monitoring, field measurements, performance analysis, and manuscript development comparing measured output with PVsyst/PVsol simulation results.

Project Info

  • Date - 2024 - 2026
  • Category - Renewable Energy, Energy Access, Research
  • System - 1.1 kWp PV, 1.5 kW inverter, 2.56 kWh storage
  • Tools - PV modelling, field instrumentation, data analysis
  • Role - Research Assistant
Functional Prototype View project

SME SolarSizer

Project Brief:

Developed a web-based solar audit and sizing tool for small and medium-sized businesses in Ghana. The application combines electricity-bill data with appliance audits to estimate energy demand, critical load, PV capacity, inverter size, battery storage, and indicative system costs. It is designed to make preliminary solar planning more accessible to non-technical users.

Project Info

  • Date - 2026
  • Category - Software, Renewable Energy, Decision Support
  • Tools - React, Tailwind CSS, JavaScript, data visualization
  • Outputs - ECG audit, solar audit, system sizing, PDF report
  • Role - Designer and developer

Project Brief:

Co-developed and operated a campus delivery service using two electric bikes to improve convenience while reducing emissions from short-distance transport.
