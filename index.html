Get to know me

Hey there! My name is Allen Kpentey. 👋🏽


I am a photgrapher and electrical engineering graduate from Ashesi University. I combine creativity and engineering to design solutions that are not only functional but also visually engaging.

I am the founder of AK Studio, where I specialize in photography and videography, creating visuals that tell powerful stories. Alongside my creative work, I am deeply passionate about renewable energy, electric vehicles, and robotics, with a focus on how technology can address Africa’s unique challenges.

I believe that STEM is a powerful driver of innovation, a tool to tackle pressing problems and unlock new opportunities. Guided by curiosity, I see myself as a lifelong learner, always eager to expand my skills, explore new technologies, and build impactful projects at the intersection of art and engineering.
